# DAUR prices

Avok Systems Inc., 16 November 2026. Prices are in US dollars, or in Canadian dollars for a buyer in Canada, and exclude any sales tax that applies.

| Option | What it covers | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Verifier | Checking any DAUR record offline in a browser | Free |
| One report | One deliverable you name within 30 days of the start date | US$1,500 (C$2,100) |
| Pilot | Up to three deliverables you name within 90 days of the start date | US$2,500 (C$3,500) |
| Annual plan | Up to 20 deliverables you name within 12 months of the start date | US$9,000 (C$12,500) |
| Firm-wide licence | Several business units or signers | On request |

A buyer that orders the annual plan within 30 days after its pilot ends pays the annual price less the pilot fee it paid.

## What every paid option includes

- An organization certificate under Avok's pilot trust list, issued after Avok checks your legal name in a public company registry, as the certificate policy in `trust/POLICY.md` sets out.
- The DAUR signing toolkit under licence for the term, with setup steps.
- A check of whether each web address, DOI and arXiv number your deliverable cites resolves. It does not test references that carry none of these, and it does not show that a source supports what the deliverable says.
- Avok's verification of each record you produce, reported to you in writing.
- A one-page note your client can follow to verify the record offline.

Your documents stay on your computers: you run the toolkit, keep your private key and decide what each record declares. Avok receives only the records you send for verification.

## Payment, refunds and term

Payment is due on order, by bank transfer, or by card where Avok sends a card payment link. Where you need a purchase order, Avok invoices and payment is due within 14 days. Avok refunds the fee in full if it ends the order before verifying your first record, or if you end the order by written notice within 30 days of the start date and before your first record is verified. Nothing renews automatically, and records you sign during the term remain verifiable after it ends.

## What a record proves

A record that verifies shows who signed which declaration about which file, and when. It does not show that the declaration is true, that the deliverable is accurate, or that you meet any rule.
