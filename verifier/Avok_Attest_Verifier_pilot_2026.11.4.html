            About
            
    
            This page checks: the file's SHA-256 against the signed record; the COSE_Sign1 signature (ES256 or Ed25519) with WebCrypto; the certificate chain to the trust list at the signing time; the RFC 3161 time-stamp (CMS signature, imprint and TSA chain); the revocation snapshot (CRLs; OCSP responses are not evaluated here), its protected commitment and the trust-list pin; retroactive invalidity dates; region hashes for byte ranges, Markdown headings and text paragraphs; and evidence files dropped beside the record, by SHA-256. PDF page and DOCX regions are reported as not recomputed in the browser; when the whole file matches, the page says the file is byte-identical to the one signed. Where the strict profile needs something this page cannot check (an OCSP-only revocation snapshot), the result is UNSUPPORTED: use the command-line verifier.
            
    
            A PASS means the record is intact, signed by a certificate the trust list accepts, and bound to this exact file. It is a declaration by the producer: not an AI-detection result, not a certification, and not a judgement of the deliverable's quality. Designed to meet Avok Attest specification v0.2.
